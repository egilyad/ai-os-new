## Summary of flags (VERIFIED)

- **EXISTS-BUT-UNUSED:** Specializations (row 5) — the three leadership competencies are never read to alter behavior.
- **UI-HIDDEN:** System prompt (4), debate persona (14), debate tactical role (15) — real but not surfaced in the agent's own panel.
- **PARTIAL:** Cognitive-event emission — works for orchestration-driven turns; DEBATE emission is contested (see 10_PROBLEMS).
- **DEAD/n-a:** No lead-specific cognitive consumer (19).
- **POTENTIAL:** Lenses (21), Coordinator routing (22), Debate moderator (23), Group leader (24) — all natural fits for a "Team Lead" that are currently absent.

## OPINION

The matrix shows a capable _generic_ agent wearing a lead costume. The highest-leverage, lowest-risk move is to convert row 5 (specializations) and row 22/23/24 (coordination) from decorative to functional — without new infra, reusing persona-selector, MetaAgentController, and groups.
